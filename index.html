    Teacher Service Commission

    The TSC collection brings together the main concepts and reference material needed for education studies and competitive examination preparation.

    Foundations and pedagogy

    • Introduction to Education: Meaning, definitions, forms, objectives, and functions of education.
    • Learning Principles: Core ideas about learning and the factors that shape it.
    • Curriculum and Books: Curriculum concepts, scope, sequence, and textbook-related notes.

    Law, policy, and administration

    • Constitution and Education: Constitutional provisions, rights, and state responsibilities.
    • Education Act 2028: Key provisions and examination-oriented summaries.
    • Education Regulation: Structure and practical notes on education regulations.
    • TSC Regulations 2057: Rules and procedures related to the Teacher Service Commission.
    • Local Government and Education: Education responsibilities and powers of local governments.
    • 16th Plan and Education: National education goals, strategies, and priorities.

    Nepal and general studies

    • History of Nepal: A chronological overview of Nepal's history.
    • History of Nepal in Nepali: Nepali-language history notes.
    • Geography of Nepal: Geography notes for general studies and examination preparation.
    • International Organisations: Reference notes on major international organisations.

    Science and Physics

    Build from foundational physical concepts to more specialized topics:

    • Mechanics: Motion, forces, energy, and foundational mechanics.
    • Quantum Physics: Notes on quantum concepts and principles.
    • Molecular Dynamics with NAMD: A practical guide for running NAMD in Google Colab.

    Linux

    The Linux section combines conceptual background with practical command-line references.

    • Linux: The Story Behind the Penguin: A historical and conceptual introduction to Linux.
    • Linux Commands: A broad command reference for everyday terminal work.
    • Linux Filesystem: Filesystem structure, paths, and storage concepts.
    • How Linux Boots: The stages involved in starting a Linux system.
    • Linux Processes and System Control: Processes, services, and system-level control.
    • Linux Miscellaneous Tasks: Practical account, hostname, and system administration tasks.

    Python

    Use these references for data analysis, visualization, and scientific programming:

    • NumPy and Pandas: Arrays, data frames, data cleaning, transformation, and analysis.
    • Matplotlib: Plotting and visualization with Python.
    • SciPy and SymPy: Scientific computing and symbolic mathematics.
